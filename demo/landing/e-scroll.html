Selected work — 02

MeetSpace

A browser-based meeting product. No install, no account, no enterprise conferencing tool for a five-minute conversation.

  • Create or join. Start a room, share a code or link. That’s the whole entry flow.
  • Check yourself first. A pre-join lobby for your name, camera and microphone.
  • Talk. Participant grid, people list, in-call chat, mute and camera controls.
  • Peer to peer. WebRTC for media, Socket.IO signalling — no media server in the path.
