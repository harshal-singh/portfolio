A bit about me

The work I like sits between the layers — how a component library is structured, what renders on the server, how the frontend and the API agree on a contract, and how any of it reaches production without someone doing a manual step at 6pm on a Friday.

That came from three pretty different jobs. Quadb meant constraint: KaiOS apps for feature phones, where a wasted kilobyte is something the user actually feels. Atrina meant ownership — an SSR rebuild, a quarter of roadmap, the onboarding docs. Webol means pace: Figma to production with AI in the loop, without dropping the review bar.

On paper: M.Sc Information Technology from KC College Mumbai (2022–2024, grade O+) and B.Sc Information Technology from Lala Lajpat Rai College (2019–2022, grade A+). Both overlapped with paid work, which is why the timeline looks a bit busy.

Right now I’m open to frontend engineering roles with product teams. Remote works well — I already keep UK hours — and I’m happy to relocate for the right team.
